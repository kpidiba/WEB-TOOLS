## 🏆 Who Is Best on the Free Plan?

| Need                                    | Winner                                        |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| ⏱️ **Longest group meeting**            | 🥇 **Jitsi Meet** (95 min)                    |
| 🚪 **Easiest (no sign-up, no install)** | 🥇 **Jitsi Meet**                             |
| 🔒 **Privacy / open source**            | 🥇 **Jitsi Meet**                             |
| 🧰 **Best all-round & most familiar**   | 🥇 **Google Meet**                            |
| 💬 **Chat + file storage included**     | 🥇 **Microsoft Teams** (5 GB, unlimited chat) |
| 📞 **Very long 1:1 calls**              | 🥇 **Microsoft Teams** (30 h)                 |
| 😬 **Shortest group limit**             | **Zoom & Webex** (40 min)                     |

### ✅ Verdict

- **Most advantages for free → Jitsi Meet.** Longest meetings, no account, open source.
- **Best polished free choice → Google Meet.** Reliable, 60 min, 100 people, works in any browser.
- **Weakest free group plans → Zoom and Webex.** 40 minutes is short.

## 🧭 Quick Picks

- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Quick call with friends or clients** → Jitsi Meet or Google Meet
- 🏫 **Class or team meeting under 1 hour** → Google Meet or Teams
- 💼 **Already use Microsoft 365** → Teams
- 📧 **Already use Gmail / Google Drive** → Google Meet
- 🔐 **Want full control and privacy** → Self-hosted Jitsi
- ⏳ **Need meetings over 1 hour, cheap** → Teams Essentials (~$4)

---

*Free limits and prices change often. Check each provider's site before you commit.*
